United States 1920–45 Wiki

A narrative-research knowledge base on American history from the aftermath of the First World War through the end of the Second World War — normalcy and the 1920s boom, Prohibition and mass culture, the Great Depression, the New Deal, industrial and labor change, neutrality and rearmament, wartime mobilization, and the Manhattan Project through 1945.
